Expand infrastructure metrics. Shops are now counted as grocery shops only, bus stops and railway stations are separate categories, and new metric families cover road accessibility (paved road distance and road density), nature objects (water bodies, forests and protected areas, including the distance to the nearest large object) and utility networks (power lines, gas and water pipelines). Object counts for water bodies, forests and protected areas are computed from merged geometries, so a single feature mapped as several OSM pieces counts once. The OpenStreetMap import now also loads linear features (roads, rails, power lines, pipelines and rivers), so the data must be re-imported.
