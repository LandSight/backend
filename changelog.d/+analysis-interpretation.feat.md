Add an interpretation module that turns a finished analysis evaluation into a human-readable report through a local GPU LLM. Rule-based services classify aspect metrics into strong/weak/neutral, pick the top metrics per aspect and the top aspects overall, then a llama.cpp-hosted model writes a Russian explanation for every aspect and a combined summary. Each generated text is verified by a grader pass, and reports are stored per analysis and returned by `GET`/`POST /api/v1/analysis/{id}/interpretation`; generation is also triggered automatically after scoring through the transactional outbox.
